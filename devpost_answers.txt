--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[1] Problem to solve
[What's the problem you want to solve?]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Modern healthcare systems and clinics suffer from three critical bottlenecks:
1. Long patient queues and inefficient manual triage leading to crowded waiting areas and delayed care.
2. Fragmented patient medical history that forces doctors to manually review and compare multiple legacy reports, losing valuable time and missing delta trends.
3. Low post-consultation compliance, as patients leave the hospital without automated, continuous, and localized reminders to take medicines or book necessary follow-up appointments.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[2] Our solution
[What are you building? Summarize the key features and how your agent functions.]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We built Smart Care AI, an autonomous multi-agent health ecosystem that automates and connects the entire patient care lifecycle:

1. Autonomous Kiosk Agent: A camera-equipped kiosk (YOLOv8 + Gemini 1.5 Pro) that automatically detects patients, performs voice-based bilingual (English & Telugu) clinical triage, and generates a smart wait-time token.
2. Doctor Insights Dashboard: A Next.js dashboard featuring speech-to-text dictation (English/Telugu mix), automatic prescription summarization, and a sliding reports panel. This panel uses Gemini Flash to perform longitudinal trend analysis (comparing multiple lab reports) and provides an interactive Clinical Reports Assistant Chat for doctors.
3. Patient Care Mobile App (React Native/Expo): A persistent patient companion that automatically tracks medicine advice, sends follow-up alerts with one-click auto-scheduling, and allows live camera/gallery lab report uploads (using Gemini Vision for instant parameters extraction).

[5] Findings and learnings
[What did you discover while building this? Any surprises, challenges, or insights worth sharing?]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Serverless State Inconsistencies: Deploying a stateful in-memory queue to Google Cloud Run exposed routing issues when scaling to multiple instances or during scale-to-zero cold starts. We resolved this by treating AI quality as a rigorous engineering discipline: we implemented JSON-based persistence (`/tmp/patient_queue.json`) and scaled Cloud Run to `--max-instances=1` to guarantee request synchronization during testing.
2. Strict Mime-Types for Agents: We discovered that when comparing multiple lab reports, instructing Gemini to reply in strict JSON schemas using `response_mime_type="application/json"` is absolutely vital to ensure output stability and avoid format parsing crashes in the frontend.
